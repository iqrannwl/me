Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The prompts are designed to help beginners communicate their ideas. Some AI tools are no-code, while more advanced changes may require technical knowledge.

Which AI tools can I use?

Use the prompts with a compatible AI website builder or coding assistant. Tool capabilities, pricing, and results vary, so check the tool's current features.

Will the prompts automatically publish my website?

No. They help you plan and generate a starting point. You still need to review the output, test functionality, and arrange hosting or publishing.

Can I use this for client projects?

You can adapt the prompts to your workflow and client requirements. Always check the terms of the AI tools you use and confirm that your final deliverables meet your client's needs.
